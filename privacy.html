Bro 2 Bro

Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

The short version
  • Bro 2 Bro is a free, private golf site for a group of friends. It has no ads and no analytics, and it never sells or rents your information.
  • We collect only what the site needs to work: your sign-in email, the name and photo you choose, your rounds and scores, your friend list and your Range distances.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time in Settings.

This policy explains what Bro 2 Bro Golf Club (“Bro 2 Bro,” “we,” “us”) collects, how it’s used and the choices you have. The site is run by Asher Baldwin in Arizona, USA, as a personal, non-commercial project.

What we collect

How we use it

  • To run the club: sign you in, keep score, work out handicaps and stats, and show leaderboards and friends.
  • To help you and fix problems: read feedback, send a password reset email when you ask for one, and keep the site safe.

We don’t use your information for advertising, sell it, rent it or share it for anyone else’s marketing.

Who can see what

  • Other members (people signed in to the club) can see your display name, photo and friend code, and the rounds and scores of rounds you’re in, along with stats built from them. The app shows you mainly your friends, but this information is available to all members.
  • Only you can see your Range bag and distances.
  • The admin (Asher) can also see feedback and the sign-in email on file for each member, so he can send password resets. He can view the app the way a member sees it, read-only. He can’t see passwords or anyone’s Range.

Services we use

These companies process data for us only to provide their service:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in and the database that stores club data. Firebase privacy
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose it. Google Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website files. GitHub Privacy Statement
  • EmailJS: delivers feedback messages to the admin’s email inbox. EmailJS Privacy Policy

The site’s fonts and photos are stored on the site itself, so loading a page doesn’t contact other font or image services. These providers may store data in the United States. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Cookies and storage on your device

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and there are no analytics. The site saves a few things in your browser so it works:

  • Your settings: light or dark theme, home state for course search and usual tees.
  • The round you’re currently scoring, so you can pick up where you left off.
  • Your email address, briefly, while you finish signing in with an emailed link.
  • Your sign-in session and an offline copy of club data from Firebase, so scoring keeps working with weak signal on the course.

If you sign in with Google, Google may set its own cookies on its sign-in pages under its own policy. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes all of the above and signs you out.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • We keep your information while your account exists.
  • Delete your account any time in Settings → Privacy & terms → Delete my account. That permanently removes your sign-in, profile, photo, saved email, Range and friend connections.
  • Rounds you played with other members stay in their history, because the scores belong to the group, but your name is removed and shown as “Former player.”
  • Feedback you sent is kept until the admin deletes it. If you’d like it removed, say so in a Feedback message before you delete your account.
  • The admin can also remove a member’s profile, for example if someone breaks the Terms.

Your choices and rights

You can turn location access for this site on or off at any time in your browser or phone settings. You can see and change your name and photo in Settings, and delete your account there too. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your data or to have it corrected or deleted. We honor those requests for everyone. Send them with the Feedback button.

Children

Bro 2 Bro isn’t meant for anyone under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we’ll delete it.

Security

The site uses HTTPS, and database rules let each member change only their own information and the rounds they’re part of. No online service is perfectly secure, though, so please use a strong password and keep your sign-in to yourself.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we’ll let members know in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests? Sign in and tap the Feedback button at the top of any page. If you can’t sign in, ask the friend who invited you to pass your message along.
